Data investigation

Wages in Alameda County

Class meeting · Compare two distributions

How do wages compare for workers with bachelor’s and master’s degrees? When the class boxplots are available, find each group’s median and interquartile range. Write one sentence comparing typical wages and another comparing the spread of the middle 50%.

Before class: Bring both sentences and a question the boxplots leave unanswered.

Data Studio · Compare the share earning over $150,000

Within each education group, divide the number earning more than $150,000 by the total number in that group. Once you have both percentages, compare them in percentage points.

Inference

Interpreting statistical evidence

Class meeting · Read both ends of a confidence interval

In one class example, 19% of drivers in the younger group and 24% in the older group came to a complete stop. The estimated difference was 5 percentage points. The 95% confidence interval ran from −0.2 to 10.2 percentage points for older minus younger drivers.

Before class: Write a sentence that describes what the two endpoints suggest about the direction and possible size of the difference.
